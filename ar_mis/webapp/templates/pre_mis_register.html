Pre-MIS Adjustment Register

Pre-MIS Outstanding never moves on its own — ordinary receipts and credit notes matching a tracked invoice have zero effect on it. It only changes when a Maker resolves a Pending Review Credit Note or Receipt as belonging to a pre-MIS-era invoice, and every such move is logged here, never silent. "Original Seed" is reconstructed as today's balance minus every adjustment on record, since the stored figure is itself a running balance, not a fixed starting point.
