What Classification means:
Current — normal in-period item, nothing to review. Shown as Current · Unapplied Nd when it's genuinely on-account and has sat unapplied for N days — the day count itself, not the label, is what tells you whether it's fresh or stale. Pending Review — this voucher's Target Doc No. didn't match any invoice this system tracks; someone needs to confirm whether it belongs to a Pre-MIS-period invoice or is a data-entry mistake. Use the Resolve control on the row to do that. Pre-MIS Adjustment — confirmed as relating to the Pre-MIS Outstanding balance; already netted against that party's Pre-MIS Outstanding and excluded from this register's own Unapplied total.
