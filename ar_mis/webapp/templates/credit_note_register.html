What Classification means:
Matched — this CN's Bill Allocation Reference correctly points to an invoice this system tracks. Nothing to review. Unapplied CN — genuinely on-account, no reference attempted at all; the same amount this register's own Unapplied CN total sums up. In Tally practice an Unapplied CN is never backdated/edited, only netted off by a fresh entry later — if one is genuinely Pre-MIS in nature and no such entry is ever coming, use its own Resolve control to confirm it as Pre-MIS Adjustment directly (its only valid resolution from here). Pending Review — this CN's Bill Allocation Reference didn't match any invoice this system tracks; someone needs to confirm whether it belongs to a Pre-MIS-period invoice or is a data-entry mistake. Use the Resolve control on the row to do that. Pre-MIS Adjustment — confirmed as relating to the Pre-MIS Outstanding balance; already netted against that party's Pre-MIS Outstanding and excluded from this register's own Unapplied total. Use Revert if this was confirmed by mistake — it reverses the exact balance move with its own logged entry and sends the row back to wherever it came from (Unapplied or Pending Review).
An Unapplied CN also gets an Unapplied Ageing bucket (0-30/31-60/.../181+) once it's sat unapplied a while — a CN unapplied 181 days is just as stale as an invoice that old, and the bucket says so.
